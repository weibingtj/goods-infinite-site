WeChat Channels Marketing for Foreign Brands

WeChat Channels is the one major video platform that lives inside China's messaging app — and the one without a cross-border storefront. This guide explains what Channels marketing is, the official rule that imported goods must clear general trade before they touch a WeChat Store, and the two routes around it.

Published 2026-11-09 · Last updated 2026-11-11 · By Bing Wei, China Market Entry & Cross-Border Commerce Specialist

WeChat Channels is the video layer inside WeChat, and it has become a selling surface

WeChat Channels marketing is the practice of using short videos and livestreams inside WeChat's Discover tab to reach buyers in the one app where they already chat, pay and repeat-purchase, so that discovery, trust and checkout sit a tap apart. The base is the largest in Chinese media: WeChat and WeChat together reached 1,418 million monthly active users at the end of 2025 (Tencent 2025 annual results). Tencent reported that Channels' total user time grew more than 20% year on year, and that rising WeChat Store transactions feed merchant technology-service revenue directly.

1,418 millionCombined WeChat and WeChat monthly active accounts at 31 December 2025, up 2% year on year — the audience every Chinese video platform competes againstSource: Tencent Holdings 2025 annual results announcement, March 2026

Most foreign brands run a WeChat Official Account; far fewer have activated the video layer of the same app, which reaches a verified, real-name audience through relationships rather than cold feed exposure.

Distribution starts from the social graph, not the algorithm

Social-graph distribution is the principle that a Channels video is shown first to the viewer's own WeChat contacts and their networks, before the algorithm widens reach on engagement signals. A share from a real contact carries a trust signal no anonymous feed impression replicates, which is why agency analyses describe roughly 60% or more of a video's initial plays as social-graph-driven (industry analysis, 2026).

Tencent's own numbers point the same way: brand-led selling on WeChat Store grew at 4.3 times the platform's overall growth rate in 2025, with monthly active merchants up 1.7 times (Tencent results coverage, March 2026). For an imported brand, a distributor's sales reps each carry a private WeChat network of buyers, and their shares of a product video reach exactly the people who reorder — a channel no influencer contract buys.

The commerce layer is now large enough to plan against

WeChat Store e-commerce is the transaction layer that Channels livestreams and short videos feed, and third-party estimates size it at roughly RMB 433 billion of GMV in 2025, up about 74.6% year on year (industry estimate, 2026). These figures are estimates rather than platform disclosures — Tencent reports service-fee growth, not GMV — but the official direction confirms it.

≈ RMB 433 billionEstimated WeChat Store e-commerce GMV in 2025, up about 74.6% year on year — an industry estimate, not a platform disclosureSource: third-party WeChat e-commerce ecosystem estimates, 2026

Category mix matters for importers: goods above RMB 300 are estimated at more than half of GMV — trust-sensitive categories where a foreign brand's provenance story actually converts.

The catch: WeChat Store has no cross-border storefront

This is the fact most entry plans miss. WeChat's own store customer service states plainly that WeChat Store "does not currently support cross-border e-commerce business; it supports sales of imported goods that cleared general trade" (WeChat Store official reply). There is no bonded-import storefront comparable to Tmall Global, JD Worldwide or Douyin Global, and no path for a Hong Kong company to open the store itself.

The published entry rules back this up: listing imported goods requires a customs import declaration issued within the past year, an inspection and quarantine certificate where the category demands one, and a delegation import agreement when the store operator is not the declaring party (WeChat Store entry rules).

Two routes for imported goods, and the entity question decides them

Route A is the cross-border route: goods import under the 1210 bonded model through Tmall Global or Douyin Global, whose storefronts are licensed for cross-border retail under the RMB 5,000 single-order and RMB 26,000 annual allowances (MOFCOM/STA, 2018). Channels serves as the media and private-domain layer that refers buyers to the storefront. Route B is the general-trade route: goods clear customs fully, a mainland entity opens the WeChat Store with the required licences, and Channels converts natively — checkout without leaving the app.

The decision is the same entity-and-import-mode decision that shapes every China plan — only it arrives one platform earlier, because Channels cannot host the bonded model itself.

The audience skews older and buys more per order

Industry audience analyses describe Channels' core buyers as roughly 30 to 50 years old, concentrated in higher-tier cities, with livestream order values above RMB 200 (industry analysis, 2026). Return rates are reported lower than on Douyin because purchase decisions filter through social trust before checkout. Every figure here is an agency estimate, but the pattern is consistent: Channels suits considered, premium purchases better than price-war live commerce.

Compliance: the livestream itself is advertising

Livestream compliance is the set of labelling, liability and record-keeping rules that attach to selling inside a broadcast. Under SAMR Order No. 72, in force since 1 May 2023, the merchant selling in a livestream is the advertiser and a named host is an advertising spokesperson, and knowledge-sharing or review content carrying a shopping link must be conspicuously marked 广告 (SAMR Order 72, 2023). AI-generated material needs a visible label and metadata marking since 1 September 2025 (CAC, 2025), purchased engagement violates the ban on traffic fraud (CAC Order 5, 2020), and advertising records must be kept for at least three years.

A 90-day entry sequence for Channels

A Channels sequence is a staged plan: decide the import route in month one, build the content engine in month two, and switch on private-domain retention in month three.

Month one is structural: choose Route A or Route B — the entity, licences and inventory position all hang on it — and line up the importer of record before any content promises availability. Month two is content: activate the Channels account against the verified Official Account, publish three to four short videos a week, and run one weekly livestream to train the algorithm. Month three is retention: move livestream viewers into WeCom groups, where repurchase and member offers run without buying reach again.

FAQ

What is WeChat Channels and why should a foreign brand use it? WeChat Channels (视频号) is the short-video and livestream product inside WeChat's Discover tab, and WeChat reached 1.418 billion monthly active users at the end of 2025. It is the only major Chinese video platform embedded in the app where buyers chat, pay and repeat-purchase, so content, storefront and customer relationships sit one tap apart.

Can a foreign brand sell cross-border goods directly on WeChat Channels? No. WeChat's own customer-service channel states that WeChat Store does not support cross-border e-commerce business and only supports imported goods that cleared general trade. Goods must be declared into China and sold by a mainland-registered entity. Brands importing under the 1210 bonded model use Tmall Global or Douyin Global as the storefront and run Channels as the media and private-domain layer.

Does a foreign brand need a Chinese entity for WeChat Channels marketing? For full commerce, yes. A WeChat Store must be opened by a mainland-registered company with category licences and complete import documentation. Media-only operations can be set up through agencies, but the account, followers and data then sit with whoever holds the credentials. Hong Kong entities can contract media work; they cannot open a WeChat Store.

How is WeChat Channels different from Douyin for a brand? Distribution is the difference. Channels starts from the viewer's WeChat social graph before the algorithm widens reach, while Douyin is algorithm-first and anonymous. Industry analyses put Channels' core audience at roughly 30–50 years old with higher average order values and lower return rates, which suits premium imported goods better than price-led live commerce.

What content rules apply on WeChat Channels? Under SAMR Order No. 72, a livestream seller is treated as the advertiser and a named host as an advertising spokesperson, and review content carrying a shopping link must be conspicuously labelled as advertising. AI-generated content needs a visible label and metadata marking since 1 September 2025, and buying engagement violates the 2020 content-ecology rules.
