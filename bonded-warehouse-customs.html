1210 vs other models

How do you pick the right China import model?

Match the model to your goal: 1210 bonded suits cross-border e-commerce with per-order tax and fast delivery; 9610 fits small-parcel exports; 0110 general trade is for bulk B2B stock with upfront duty. Most first-time entrants start with 1210 via an import agent, no local entity needed.
