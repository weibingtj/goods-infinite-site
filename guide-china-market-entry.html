4 · Bonded warehousing & 1210 fulfillment

1210 is China's cross-border e-commerce bonded import model: goods sit in a China bonded zone and clear per order, so you pay tax only when a unit actually sells. It is the default for Tmall Global, JD Worldwide and most cross-border storefronts.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a Chinese company to sell into China?

Not for a first cross-border test. A Hong Kong trading entity or a licensed import agent can act as importer of record under the 1210 model. A WFOE becomes relevant when you scale into domestic-store (Tmall/JD domestic) distribution or certain regulated categories. See Hong Kong entity for China imports.

What is the fastest compliant path to first sale?

For many brands: Hong Kong entity + 1210 bonded import + Tmall Global cross-border store. A representative European skincare brand reached first sale in 9 weeks this way, with no WFOE. See case studies.

How much does it cost?

GOODSINFINITE works open-book: the factory price is shown as-is and the service fee is stated separately (typically 3–8% of goods value, or a fixed project fee of $1,000–5,000). See pricing and open-book pricing.

Which platform should I start on?

It depends on category, brand maturity and fulfilment model. Tmall Global suits established brands; JD Worldwide signals trust and logistics strength; Douyin cross-border rides content-driven discovery. Compare them in Tmall Global vs. JD Worldwide vs. Douyin.
