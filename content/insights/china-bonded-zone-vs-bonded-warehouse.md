## A bonded zone is a fenced area inside China's customs territory where imported goods can be held before import duty is paid — and the duty falls due only when the goods leave for domestic sale

Bonding is a status, not a promise of exemption. Under Art. 36 of the [Tariff Law of the PRC](https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202404/content_6947843.htm) (adopted 26 April 2024, in force 1 December 2024), bonded goods that are re-exported are exempt from customs duty, while bonded goods that are not re-exported and are instead sold into the domestic market are dutiable under the applicable rules. Art. 21(1) fixes the rate: for bonded goods sold domestically, the applicable tariff rate is the one in force on the day the tax formalities are completed, not the day the goods arrived.

That single mechanism is the commercial point of bonding. Working capital stays in inventory instead of sitting in duty, duty is paid only on units actually sold, and unsold stock can be re-exported without ever having been taxed. The trade-off is supervision: under Art. 34 of GACC Decree No. 256, an enterprise in the zone must hold market-entity status and register or record with customs, and every movement in and out is recorded.

## Four legal places your inventory can sit in China — and three of them are regulated by different customs decrees

Chinese law recognises several distinct storage statuses, and confusing them is the most common planning error we see. A comprehensive bonded zone is an area; a bonded warehouse is a facility; a type-B bonded logistics centre is a multi-client facility that sits outside a zone; an ordinary warehouse holds only goods that have already cleared and been taxed.

Scales differ sharply. Setting up a type-B bonded logistics centre requires a storage area of at least 50,000 m² in eastern China and 20,000 m² in central, western and north-eastern China under Art. 4 of [GACC Decree No. 130](http://www.customs.gov.cn/customs/attachDir/2026/01/2026010614445051917.pdf), with applications received by the direct-administered customs and approved by GACC. A bonded warehouse is a far smaller approval handled at direct-administered customs level.

## The storage clock is the difference that decides the model: one year in a bonded warehouse, no statutory limit in a comprehensive bonded zone

Bonded warehouse goods run on a hard clock. Under [GACC Decree No. 105](http://www.customs.gov.cn/eportal/fileDir/customs/resource/cms/2018/05/2018053017424187525.doc), the storage period for bonded goods is one year; where there is a proper justification, customs may agree an extension, and except in special circumstances the extension may not exceed one year.

Comprehensive bonded zones work differently. Art. 33 of [GACC Decree No. 256](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2022/content_5682420.htm) states that, unless another law or regulation provides otherwise, **goods in the zone are not subject to a storage period**. If you are building a regional distribution hub with slow-moving SKUs, that single article is usually decisive.

## What happens on the way out is where the money is: actual state, or selectively by input materials

Four exit paths carry four different tax consequences under Decree No. 256. Goods leaving for overseas are exempt from export duty (Art. 15). Goods moving between a zone and another special supervision area or bonded supervision site remain bonded, with no customs duty or import-stage taxes levied on the transfer (Arts. 25–26). Goods leaving for the domestic market are taxed according to their actual state at the time of exit — but Art. 18 gives a choice for goods processed in the zone: duty may instead be assessed on the corresponding imported input materials, with deferred-duty interest, while import-stage taxes follow the actual state of the goods. And Art. 19 preserves preferential treatment: goods moving out to the domestic market under a preferential trade agreement may receive the agreement or special preferential rate if the applicable rules of origin are met.

## What you can do inside the zone: twelve permitted activities, and one hard limit in a bonded warehouse

Art. 5 of Decree No. 256 lists what zone enterprises may carry out: research and development, processing, manufacturing and remanufacturing; testing and repair; storage of goods; logistics distribution; financial leasing; cross-border e-commerce; commodity exhibition; international entrepot trade; international transit; port operations; bonded delivery of futures; and other activities the state permits in the zone. Two further provisions matter commercially: Art. 42 allows a value-added-tax general taxpayer pilot inside the zone, which is what lets a zone enterprise sell into the domestic VAT chain, and Art. 43 gives simplified in-and-out handling for domestic goods entering the zone that involve no export duty, no licence, no refund claim and no customs statistics.

A bonded warehouse is much narrower. Decree No. 105 permits simple processing only — packaging, grading and classifying, marking, splitting and assembly — and prohibits substantial processing. Goods may not be sold, transferred, pledged or otherwise disposed of without customs approval.

## Where 1210 cross-border e-commerce stock must sit: inside a special supervision area or a type-B bonded logistics centre, and nowhere else

The 1210 model has a location requirement, not just a licence requirement. Under the [six-ministry circular Shang Cai Fa [2018] No. 486](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-12/31/content_5437823.htm), bonded online-shopping imports are not, in principle, permitted to operate outside a customs special supervision area: the circular states that the bonded online-purchase plus offline self-pickup model may not be run outside such an area. Commerce authorities describe the stocking step the same way - goods move in bulk into a customs special supervision area or a type-B bonded logistics centre ([Wuhai commerce bureau comparison of the 1210, 1239, 9610 and 9710/9810 flows](http://swj.wuhai.gov.cn/swj/843439/843564/843569/843637/1430328/index.html)) - after which the consumer orders and the goods clear out of the area for domestic delivery. A commercial warehouse outside them cannot hold 1210 stock, which is why the "bonded warehouse" on a Tmall Global listing page is a facility inside a zone or a type-B centre — see [What Is 1210 Bonded Import?](https://www.goods-infinite.com/insights/what-is-1210-bonded-import.html) and [China Bonded-to-Consumer Delivery](https://www.goods-infinite.com/insights/china-bonded-to-consumer-delivery.html) for the order-level mechanics.

The State Council has pushed the two together: [Guo Fa [2019] No. 3](https://www.huidong.gov.cn/zfxxgkml/hdxrmzfmhwz/gzwj/content/post_1129427.html) sets the goal of progressively making cross-border e-commerce retail import policy fully applicable across comprehensive bonded zones, as part of 21 measures built around five target roles — processing and manufacturing, R&D and design, logistics distribution, testing and repair, and sales and service.

## Five questions that decide where your stock should sit

**First, will the goods be sold one order at a time to Chinese consumers?** If yes and the product is on the cross-border retail import list, 1210 requires a zone or type-B centre, and per-order value stays within the RMB 5,000 single-transaction and RMB 26,000 annual caps. **Second, will the stock sit for more than a year?** If yes, the one-year bonded-warehouse clock is a liability and a zone is the safer home. **Third, will you process, repack or repair in China?** Substantial processing needs a zone; a bonded warehouse permits only simple processing. **Fourth, will some of the stock be re-exported?** Re-exported bonded goods are duty-exempt under Art. 36 of the Tariff Law, which is worth real money on regional distribution models. **Fifth, do you need VAT invoices into the domestic market?** That depends on whether your zone partner can operate under the general taxpayer pilot in Art. 42, and on whether you have a Chinese taxable entity at all.

## The city matters as much as the model: zones are national policy delivered locally

Zone rules are national; clearance speed and last-mile reach are local. Tianjin is a worked example: the [State Council's master plan for the China (Tianjin) Pilot Free Trade Zone](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2015-04/20/content_9625.htm) (Guo Fa [2015] No. 19) covers 119.9 km² across three areas, including 10 km² of Dongjiang bonded port area, 1.96 km² of Binhai New Area comprehensive bonded zone and 4 km² of bonded logistics park, and it directs the customs special supervision areas inside the FTZ to focus on trade facilitation. The [2018 deepening plan](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2018-05/24/content_5293012.htm) (Guo Fa [2018] No. 14) adds operational detail, including support for online shopping bonded imports to be sorted before declaration when entering a special supervision area, and removal of the bonded storage time limit for parallel-import vehicles.

## FAQ

**Is a bonded zone the same thing as a bonded warehouse in China?** No. A comprehensive bonded zone is a fenced customs special supervision area governed by GACC Decree No. 256, where registered zone enterprises carry out twelve categories of activity and goods have no statutory storage period under Art. 33. A bonded warehouse is a single licensed facility governed by GACC Decree No. 105 with a one-year storage period extendable by up to one year. A type-B bonded logistics centre under Decree No. 130 is a third form: one operator, many users.

**Does putting goods in a bonded zone mean I avoid import duty?** No — it defers it. Art. 36 of the Tariff Law provides that bonded goods re-exported are exempt from customs duty, while bonded goods sold domestically are dutiable; Art. 21(1) sets the rate at the day tax formalities are completed.

**How long can goods stay in bonded storage in China?** Bonded warehouse goods: one year, extendable by up to one year with customs agreement. Zone goods: no statutory storage period under Art. 33 of Decree No. 256.

**Where must 1210 cross-border e-commerce stock be stored?** Inside a customs special supervision area or a type-B bonded logistics centre. Customs guidance on Shang Cai Fa [2018] No. 486 states the 1210 business can only be conducted in those locations.

**Can a foreign brand rent bonded space in China without a Chinese company?** Not in its own name. Art. 34 of Decree No. 256 requires market-entity status and customs registration for zone enterprises, and bonded storage operators must themselves be approved and registered by customs, so the stock sits under the record of an importer of record while the brand retains commercial ownership until declaration.

**What can I do to goods while they are inside a bonded zone?** Art. 5 of Decree No. 256 permits twelve activities including processing, remanufacturing, testing and repair, distribution, exhibition and cross-border e-commerce. A bonded warehouse permits only simple processing under Decree No. 105 and prohibits substantial processing.
