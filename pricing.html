FAQ

Common questions

How is your fee structured?

Factory price is shown exactly as quoted by the supplier. Our service fee is stated separately — a fixed project fee or a percentage of goods value. There is no hidden rebate.

What does bonded fulfillment cost?

Bonded storage is billed monthly by space used; customs clearance and 1210 per-order fulfillment are billed per shipment or per piece. Exact rates depend on SKU size and volume.

Do I need to set up a Chinese company?

Often not. Many brands import under our Hong Kong trading entity and sell via cross-border e-commerce (1210). A WFOE is only needed for certain categories or deeper local presence.
